STOCKPILOT 2.0 for macOS (Apple Silicon: M1 / M2 / M3 / M4) - a mod for Screen Stocks

Install (easiest):
  Right-click "Install StockPilot.command" and choose Open (then Open again).
  On macOS 15 or newer: if it says it can't be verified, open System Settings >
  Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway".
  StockPilot is copied to Applications and starts. It's also in Launchpad.

Or drag StockPilot into the Applications folder shown here. If macOS then says
StockPilot "is damaged" or can't be opened, use the Install command above instead
(StockPilot isn't from the App Store, and the installer clears that warning).

Uninstall: right-click "Uninstall StockPilot.command" > Open.
The game must be open for trades to happen.
