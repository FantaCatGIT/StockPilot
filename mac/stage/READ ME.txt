STOCKPILOT v1.1 - MAC (a mod for Screen Stocks)

1. Drag "StockPilot" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't
   from the App Store: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and
   click "Open Anyway" (older macOS: right-click the app > Open).
3. First start downloads PowerShell once (about 70 MB), then the panel opens in
   your browser window. It turns the game's mod support on for you.

Stop the bot with the Stop button in the panel. To remove everything, open
"Uninstall StockPilot".
